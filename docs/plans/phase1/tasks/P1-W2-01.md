## Acceptance: independently tested on the real app
1. Decode split UTF-8 sequences, emoji/combining characters, arrows, Escape, Kitty replies and bracketed paste; byte-to-char conversion is forbidden.
2. Paste remains inert draft text, never slash-command execution or submission, including embedded newlines and escape/control bytes.
3. Bound ingress queue and paste bytes; coalesce resize/redraw without dropping mandatory input or authorization decisions.
4. Quit, Ctrl-C, EOF, startup failure, recoverable panic and supported suspend/resume restore prior terminal modes; renderer and reader tasks are canceled/joined.
