Status: COMPLETE. Kind: product.

## User-observable outcome

Format-2 installation catalog initializes from scratch on empty files,
refuses to overwrite existing data, verifies blake3 migration checksum
on reopen, supports concurrent readers.

- `crates/storage/src/schema_v2.rs` (SchemaV2 workspace init/reopen):
  protocol reused.

## Observable contract

- initialize_catalog(path): fail-closed on non-empty files; sets
  application_id ORC2, user_version 2, WAL mode, seeds catalog_state
  and schema_migrations with blake3 checksum.
- open_existing(path): verifies application_id, user_version,
  schema_migrations version + checksum; fails closed on mismatch.
- No overwrite on reopen; no unbounded state.
