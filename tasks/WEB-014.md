# WEB-014 - Chat navigation, search and long-history UX

Status: NOT STARTED. Kind: product. Runtime optional: False.
Mandatory for full declared release: yes.
Requirements: REQ-040.
Dependencies: session query/history APIs.
Test obligations: WEB-014-T01, WEB-014-T02, WEB-014-T03, WEB-014-T04, WEB-014-T05.

## User-observable outcome

The sidebar supports pinning, unified chat search, archive/share actions, temporary
chat and progressively paged long histories without loading an entire account or
conversation into memory.

## Observable contract and tests

- **WEB-014-T01 — navigation/search:** pin/unpin, title/content search, archive/share
  and progressive history loading operate on real persisted sessions/messages.
- **WEB-014-T02 — temporary/private state:** temporary chat is explicitly non-durable
  or durably marked according to its contract; unavailable sharing/search states are
  not silently converted into ordinary persisted chat.
- **WEB-014-T03 — accessibility/shortcuts:** sidebar/search/paging/action controls are
  keyboard navigable with discoverable shortcuts and correct focus after navigation.
- **WEB-014-T04 — history bounds:** session search and message paging are bounded,
  cancellable and virtualizable; the recent provider-context window does not reuse
  the known legacy oldest-page bug.
- **WEB-014-T05 — reload/order fidelity:** pins, archive state, search results and
  progressively loaded message ordering remain stable after reload.
