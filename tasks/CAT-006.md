## Observable contract

- `CatalogStats`: fields `total_plugins`, `loaded_count`, `error_count` (all `u64`).
- `record_loaded()`: increments `loaded_count` by 1.
- `record_error()`: increments `error_count` by 1.
- `save_stats(path: &Path, &CatalogStats) -> io::Result<()>`: persist as JSON.
- `load_stats(path: &Path) -> io::Result<CatalogStats>`: load from JSON.
- `stats_summary(&CatalogStats) -> String`: human-readable summary with counts.
