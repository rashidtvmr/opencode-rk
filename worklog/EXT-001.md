## Contract decisions

- `PluginId(u64)` monotonic from 1; `SUPPORTED_CONTRACT_VERSION: u32 = 1`; name/cap charset `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]*`; caps `MAX_PLUGINS=64`, `MAX_CAPABILITIES=16`, `MAX_NAME_LEN=128`, `MAX_CAP_LEN=64`.
- `add` validates name/version/caps, rejects dup-name and overflow, inserts `Registered`, consumes no id on error. `mark_ready` flips to `Ready` (unknown => `Unknown`). `remove` only that id (unknown => `false`). `wait_ready` parks 1 ms slices, checks cancel at least every 1 ms (within 50 ms), unknown/removed => `Unknown`. `list()` sorted by id.
- Caller owns registry lifetime + `AtomicBool` cancel; `wait_ready` takes `&self`, spawns no thread. No wall-clock in ids/ordering. Declarations hold names/labels only.
