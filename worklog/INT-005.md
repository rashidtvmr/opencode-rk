## Alias decision (2026-09-15 lane: INT-005/006/007)

- Added additive-only card-name aliases to `int_refresh.rs` (no logic change):
  `CredId=CredKey`, `RefreshState=IntRefreshState`, `RefreshGate=IntRefreshGate`,
  `RefreshGuard=IntRefreshGuard`, `RefreshError=IntRefreshError`,
  `RefreshFail=IntRefreshFail`, `needs_refresh->needs_int_refresh`,
  `REFRESH_WINDOW_MS=INT_REFRESH_WINDOW_MS(300_000)`,
  `MAX_INFLIGHT=INT_REFRESH_MAX(128)`, `MAX_CRED_BYTES=INT_MAX_CRED_BYTES`,
  plus `IntRefreshState::credential_id()` accessor.
- Rationale: sibling `refresh_gate.rs` already owns exact card names
  (`RefreshState`/`CredId`/etc.); reusing them in `int_refresh.rs` would collide
  at `lib.rs` scope. Aliases resolve card names to `Int*` impls; lib scoping
  (`int_refresh::RefreshGate` vs `refresh_gate::RefreshGate`) keeps both live.
  Old `Int*` names kept; frozen tests untouched.
- Field deviation retained: struct literal field stays `credential`
  (card says `credential_id`); accessor bridges reads.
- Also pre-wired `pub mod int_refresh;` into `crates/providers/src/lib.rs`
  (integrator-owned file; additive one-line only).
